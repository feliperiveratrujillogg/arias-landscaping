Our Services

Landscaping Services for Your Property

Lawn care, cleanups, and landscaping work for homes and properties in the Savannah area — handled by one team.

Lawn Mowing, Edging & Trimming

Regular mowing with clean edges along walks and driveways and trimmed borders, so your lawn looks neat and professionally maintained.

Bush & Hedge Trimming

Shaping and trimming bushes and hedges to keep them clean, controlled, and in proportion with the rest of your landscaping.

Yard Cleanups

Clearing out overgrown, messy, or neglected outdoor areas and getting the property back to a clean, manageable state.

Mulch & Flower Bed Installation

Refreshing flower beds and landscape areas with clean, evenly installed mulch and well-defined bed edges.

Flowers & Shrubs

Planting flowers and shrubs to add color and structure and improve the overall look of your property's landscaping.

Pine Straw Installation

Fresh pine straw laid in beds and around trees for a clean, finished look that's common throughout Savannah landscapes.

Weekly Lawn Maintenance

For customers who want consistent, recurring care so the property stays sharp week to week without having to think about it.

Biweekly Lawn Maintenance

Service every other week for properties that need dependable upkeep on a less frequent schedule.
